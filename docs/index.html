How it works

  1. Fetch

    Pull every repo created in the last 14 days with 40 or more stars from the GitHub search API, plus a baseline of repos from the 60 days before with 150 or more.

  2. Split into terms

    Turn each repo into terms: words from its name and description, plus its topics. Repos whose terms match another owner's repo at 60% or more are set aside as clones.

  3. Find bursts

    For each term, divide its recent repos by the number the baseline predicts. A term needs 5 or more repos and a burst of 3 or more.

  4. Merge into waves

    Merge bursting terms whose repos overlap by 40% or more. Each group is a wave, ranked by stars per day, with 14 days of star history.

  5. Flag

    Flag a wave when 60% or more of its repos were created on one day, when it has fewer owners than half its repos, when its top repo holds under 10% of the stars, or when it is built from clones.
